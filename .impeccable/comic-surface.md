## Direction contract

THESIS: the page image is the reading surface; controls stay on its perimeter. The editor exposes the actual upload sequence and page order, rather than decorating a generic dashboard.

OWN-WORLD: explicit later user screenshots pin the TT reader to Comix graphite/teal (#222a2c/#293235) and cyan (#5de4f4), compact 36px desktop icon targets and centered dialogs. Keep existing self-hosted Be Vietnam Pro. Editor stays warm paper/ink with restrained vermilion and ordered thumbnails. This overrides the first provisional brass reader palette, without redesigning VI/EN.

STORY: choose TT, read the published illustrated edition, open chapters or reading settings, and return to the same text chapter. In the authenticated editor, inspect ordered images, save a private shared draft, preview it, then explicitly publish or withdraw. Public reading never loads device drafts.

FIRST VIEWPORT: reader image starts at the viewport top, 50% viewport width on desktop, fit-width default on phone. Floating VI/EN/TT at top left, a one-line chapter box and compact right rail, stretch/zoom only at bottom right. No thumbnail/page-count furniture. Settings dialog 480px and chapters 560px, centered with a dark backdrop. Editor has book/chapter rail, upload canvas, ordered page grid and inspector. Phone preserves 44px touch targets while icons remain small.

FORM: assigned reference-led reading stage and upload workbench, code-led extension; seed key local-tt-20261030. No replacement of the existing visual world.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.
