# TT sample artwork

Original illustrative samples generated with the built-in **image_gen** tool, with the user's approval to use sample images. No Comix/MangaDex/Mangapill chapter images are included.

- `demo-01.png`: traveling scholar at a temple, four panels.
- `demo-02.png`: moonlit pavilion and poet, four panels.
- Exact prompts and generation identifiers: `docs/assets/comic-demo-prompts.json` in the local project.
- The PNG files carry the same prompt/source metadata in an iTXt `Description` chunk. Pixel data is unchanged from generation.

These pages demonstrate the upload/reader interface. They are not the provided book's original images or a complete adaptation of its text.
