#1
Nhân quả rõ ràng, giống hệt tiếng vang, hình bóng = When cause brings forth effect, it follows as surely as shadow follows form and echo follows sound
Ngẫm lẽ ấy vốn là rõ rệt = This principle has always been plain to see
Nhưng con người thật quá ngoan ngu = Alas, that men should remain so obstinate and benighted
Ghen tức nhiều điều = They vie with one another in wrath and rancor
Riêng tây lắm chuyện = They vainly set self against other
Chìm sông ngã giếng, đắm đuối càng sâu = Like men fallen into a well, they sink ever deeper into the churning depths
Lấp giếng vùi hầm, xô bồ đã lắm = Like men tumbling into a pit, they are everywhere, and the whole world teems with their kind
U trầm đến vậy = So deeply have they sunk into the gloom
Đọa lạc càng thương = So lost and fallen, they are truly to be pitied
Vì thế mà, trời có đường bạt độ, tỉnh thức người mê = Hence the Nine Heavens hand down the code of deliverance, to rouse the deluded and awaken those who walk in darkness
Đất có ngục luân hồi, khuyên răn kẻ xấu = And the Ten Courts of the Underworld keep the prisons of transmigration, to punish wrongs already done and deter those yet to come
Lỗi mà chẳng đổi = Yet there are those who, even so, will not repent
Tội ắt không dong = Upon them, punishment must surely fall
Nay Lý mỗ, sâu cát thân hèn = Now, this Ly So-and-so is of no more worth than insects and sand
Kiến giun phận mọn = He has a body no better than an ant's or a louse's
Kết bạn thì mây mưa tráo trở = In his friendships he was fickle beyond measure, turning clouds to rain with a flick of the hand
Cư tâm thì yêu quái gớm ghê = In the depths of his heart lurked goblins and vengeful ghosts
Văn học xem khinh = He was as ill-suited to letters and learning as a square tenon to a round mortise
Bạc tiền coi trọng = Goods and riches he prized as though they were hills and mountains
Chiếm cướp ruộng người như Hồng Dương đời Hán (7) = He seized other men's fields like the Marquis of Hongyang of the Han (7)
Giết hại mạng người như Dương Tố đời Tùy(8) = In unbridled slaughter he outdid Yang Su of the Sui (8)
Vu oan giá họa, so hùm beo gấu sói còn độc hơn = In destroying men and stirring up calamity, he surpassed even jackals, wolves, and savage beasts
Cực dục cùng xa, dù khe suối núi gò chưa đủ thỏa = In feeding his lusts and indulging every extravagance, he could not be sated, not even by bottomless ravines or towering mountains
Chỉ bởi lòng tham sai khiến = When all is said, it was greed that drove him
Thực tuồng gian hoạt tót vời = Truly, he is the very chief of villains
Phải dùng phép nghiêm = Should he not be dealt with under the sternest of statutes
Làm răn kẻ khác = Let all who come after take warning from him
