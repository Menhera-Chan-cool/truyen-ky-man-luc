#0
ức tích bình sinh nhật = I recall the days of our life together
Tăng hài khế hợp nhân = When we were bound in wedded harmony
Cảm quân tình thái hậu = I was moved by how deep your love ran
Tiếu ngã mệnh chung truân = And laughed that my fate held nothing but hardship
Biệt quệ phân huề tảo = Too soon we parted, letting go each other's hands
Trường đình khuyến ẩm tần = And at the long pavilion you pressed wine on me again and again
Y y sầu lĩnh kiệu = Sorrow lingered on the mountain passes
Nhiễu nhiễu cách phong trần = And endless wind and dust kept us apart
Cộng ước nhân thiên lý = We kept our vow across a thousand leagues
Tương vương nguyệt bán luân = And looked toward each other under the half-moon
Xâm tầm nhàn lục tải = Six aimless years slipped quickly by
Linh tạ trướng song thân = And I grieved as both my parents withered and fell
Phạ thụy Hoành sơn hiểu = Some dawns I lay down to sleep on Hoanh Mountain
Hành ca Diễn thủy tân = Some days I walked singing by the ford of the Dien
Đăng lâu Vương Xán lệ = Climbing the tower, I wept the tears of Wang Can
Xách cú Đỗ Lăng cân = Hunting for verses, I wore Duling's kerchief
Trúc thạch nan y tục = Bamboo and stone could hardly cure my coarse ways
Cầm tôn bất liệu bần = And with lute and wine cup I took no heed of being poor
Tha hương lao ký mục = In a strange land I strained my eyes in vain
Cố quốc trọng thương thần = While my homeland weighed on my wounded spirit
Phóng lãng phi ngô sự = A wastrel's life was never my true calling
Yêm lưu bệnh thử thân = Yet staying away so long has worn this body down
Ninh tri Bồng Đảo khách = Who could have known the guest of Penglai Isle
Dao dạt Cẩm Giang lân = Would send word from afar to her neighbor by the Brocade River
Thái Thạch trùng di trạo = At Caishi I turned my oar back once more
Hoàng Cô lưỡng vấn tân = At Huanggu I asked the way to the ford a second time
Kỷ niên Vu Giáp mộng = For years I dreamed of the Wu Gorge
Nhất đán Vũ Lăng xuân = Then in one morning came the spring of Wuling
Hồ điệp giao tình cựu = Like butterflies we took up our old affection
Uyên ương biến thái tân = Like mandarin ducks we found it fresh again
Khinh huyên Đường Quắc quốc = Lightly adorned, like the Lady of Guo in Tang
Mỹ mạn Tống Đông lân = Lovely as the girl next door in Song
Lục ám oanh thanh sáp = The green grows dense and the orioles' song turns rough
Hồng hy yến tử sân = The red grows sparse and the swallows fret
Hiệp du kim Đỗ Mục = In gallant roving, I am a Du Mu(11) of today
Kỳ ngộ cổ Lưu Thần = In this wondrous meeting, I am a Liu Chen of old
Ngàm vịnh liêu tùy hứng = I chant my verses as the mood takes me
Phong lưu khẳng nhượng nhân = In romance, would I give way to any man
Hội ưng truyền thắng sự = So fine a tale deserves to be handed down
Mệnh bút ký Chu Tần = So I take up the brush to record this wedded bond

Hỡi ơi nương tử = Alas, my lady
Khuê nghi đáng bậc = In womanly bearing, a model to all
Hiền đức vẹn mười = In goodness and virtue, perfect and whole
Tinh thần nhã đạm = Your spirit was graceful and serene
Dáng điệu xinh tươi = Your manner lovely and bright
Khi về với ta = When you came to be my bride
Vợ chồng thân thiết = As husband and wife we were close and dear
Ai biết giữa đường = Who could have known that midway on our path
Phút nên ly biệt = We would so suddenly be parted
Cha làm quan xa = My father took office in a distant land
Ta theo hầu hạ = And I went with him to serve
Trải sáu năm dư = More than six years went by
Bặt tin nhạn cá = Without a word by goose or carp

Hạc oán vượn sầu = The crane lamented, the gibbon grieved
Than ôi đường trước = Alas, the road you traveled
Gieo neo đến đâu = How hard and lonely it must have been
Bên giời góc bể = At the edge of the sky, at the corner of the sea
Nệm khách lẻ loi = A traveler lay alone upon his mat
Tin nhà chợt đến = Then tidings from home came of a sudden
Ngựa về quất roi = And I lashed my horse to hasten back
Sắt cầm dìu dặt = Once more the zither and the lute played in sweet accord
Lại gắn keo loan = Once more the phoenix glue mended the broken strings
Vừa vui sum họp = Scarcely had we rejoiced in our reunion
Phút bỗng lìa tan = When in an instant we were torn apart
Ta sao bạc quá = How faithless I have been
Nàng đáng thương thay = How pitiable your fate
Nói năng gì nữa = What more is there to say
Đã đến nỗi này = Now that it has come to this
Hoa bay trước viện = Blossoms drift before the courtyard
Quế rụng giữa trời = The cassia falls from the midst of heaven
Phù dung ủ rũ = The hibiscus droops and withers
Dương liễu tả tơi = The willows hang tattered and torn
Phong cảnh còn đây = The scenery remains as it was
Người đã xa chơi = But you have wandered far away
Lấy gì độ em = With what shall I deliver your soul
Một lễ lên chùa = With offerings at the temple
Lấy gì khuây em = With what shall I console you
Duyên sau đền bù = With a bond renewed in lives to come
Non mòn bể cạn = Though mountains wear away and seas run dry
Mối hận khôn khuây = This sorrow shall never be eased

Hâm hưởng lễ này = Deign to partake of this offering
