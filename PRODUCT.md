# Truyền kỳ mạn lục
<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->
## Platform
web
## Stack
Approved: Next.js, TypeScript, Supabase, Vercel.
## Users and purpose
Public Vietnamese and English readers of one book. Three or four invited editors publish the owner's prepared translations and illustrations. Only editors sign in. Guest progress and notes stay on the device.
## Capabilities and constraints
The approved implementation plan is the specification. Import EPUB/DOCX/TXT/HTML/PDF, preview selected chapters, edit visual/HTML, maintain drafts and publication history, invite/revoke editors. VI/EN changes interface and supplied book text together. No automatic book translation, OCR, library catalogue, reader accounts, or ebook export.
## Evidence
Vietnamese EPUB in Downloads; twenty English DOCX files in D:/truyenkimanluc. Author metadata is Nguyễn Dữ. Reference projects are EPUB-Forge and Novel-Hub, MIT licensed.
## Principles
Protect originals. Never expose drafts. Never silently replace edits. Read without an account. Be honest about browser-dependent speech and PDF limitations.
